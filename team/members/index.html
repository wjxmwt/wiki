01

Project Leader

Jiahua Weng

TEAM LADER & WETLAB LEADER

“ Every small breakthrough taught me rigor, patience, and the resolve never to give up.”


When I first joined the iGEM experimental team, I was eager to bring our bold designs to life in the laboratory. However, the journey proved far more challenging than I had expected, with repeated failed transformations, faint gel bands, and inconsistent data. I often spent long hours at the workbench, troubleshooting experiments and refining our approaches. This experience not only strengthened my practical laboratory skills but also taught me the resilience and persistence required of a researcher. It showed me that scientific progress often comes through patience, problem-solving, and the willingness to learn from failure.
