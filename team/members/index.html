Project Leader

Jiahua Wong

TEAM LADER & WETLAB MEMBER

“ Every small breakthrough taught me rigor, patience, and the resolve never to give up.”


When I first joined the iGEM experimental team, I was full of anticipation to realize our bold designs in the lab. Yet the journey turned out far tougher than expected: repeated failed transformations, faint gel bands, and erratic fluctuating data. I often spent long hours by the workbench, troubleshooting non‑stop. This experience not only honed my practical laboratory skills but also helped me understand the resilience a researcher ought to possess.
